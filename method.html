The Mile One method

Launch is mile one.

Most teams train for launch day and stop running. But a launch is a marathon: the start line is where the real race begins. Mile One is my plan for the 90 days after launch, when the message either lands or quietly dies.

The PMM Playbook 4 checkpoints · 90 days
01
Day 0 · The start line

Start strong, start measured

Launch day is mile one, not the finish line. Ship the story, and set the baselines you'll judge it by before anyone asks "did it work?"

  • One-line positioning everyone can repeat: Product, Marketing and Sales
  • Baselines for adoption, conversion and pipeline, written down before launch
  • Sellers enabled with a story, not a 40-slide deck
You're on pace if: every team describes the launch in the same sentence.
02
Day 30 · Check your splits

Read the signals

Adoption, site search and feedback tell you whether the message landed. Look before you add features.

  • Adoption and activation versus your Day 0 baseline
  • What people search for, click on and drop off at
  • What sellers hear on calls, and what they've stopped saying
You're on pace if: the buyer's words start matching your positioning.
03
Day 60 · Mid-race

Adjust the pace

Turn the signals into hypotheses and test them. Fix the message, the page or the pitch before you blame the product.

  • "Here's what I'd test": 2–3 hypotheses ranked by impact
  • A/B tests on the pages and moments where buyers hesitate
  • Refresh talk tracks with what's winning in the field
You're on pace if: at least one test is live and the pipeline trend is visible.
04
Day 90 · The finish line

Finish with revenue, then share the story

Close the loop: report impact in the language leadership cares about, roll the winners into the next launch and tell the story so the whole company learns from it.

  • Impact on pipeline, conversion and ARR, not just activity
  • What we'd keep, cut and test in the next launch
  • A story worth retelling: the hook, the twist and the lesson
You've finished if: the next launch starts from what this one taught you.
