The five

Five things you get with me that you don't get with most PMMs

Plenty of product marketers can write a positioning doc. Here's what's different about how I work.

01

I test my own thesis

I started in CRO, so messaging doesn't ship on gut feel. Positioning goes into A/B tests, behavioral data and real buyer reactions before it scales.

Most PMMs: hand over a messaging doc and hope it lands.
02

Launch is mile one

I plan the 90 days after launch day: reading the adoption signals, fixing what didn't land and refueling sales. That's where most of the revenue is decided.

Most PMMs: treat launch day as the finish line.
03

I speak engineer

A BS in Computer Science plus IBM certifications in AI and Data Science means I can sit with product and engineering, get the real "how", and turn it into value buyers care about.

Most PMMs: translate the feature list, not the value.
04

Stories, not slides

A hook, the twist, the lesson. I build enablement the way I make content, so 250+ sellers actually use it and it shows up in closed deals and QBRs.

Most PMMs: ship a 40-slide deck nobody opens twice.
05

I've owned the number

At Crain I owned a subscription portfolio's growth and led a team of 4. I think in pipeline, conversion and ARR, not just in launches shipped.

Most PMMs: report activity, not revenue.
